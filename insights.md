# Customer Churn Analysis - Business Insights

## Executive Summary

An analysis of 7,043 telecom customers was conducted to understand customer churn behavior, identify high-risk customer segments, and estimate business impact.

The study reveals that contract type, payment method, internet service, and tenure significantly influence customer churn.

---

# Key Findings

## 1. Contract Type

Customers with Month-to-Month contracts show the highest churn.

Long-term contracts (One-Year and Two-Year) significantly improve customer retention.

Recommendation:

- Encourage customers to upgrade to annual plans.
- Offer discounts for long-term subscriptions.

---

## 2. Payment Method

Customers using Electronic Check exhibit the highest churn compared to other payment methods.

Recommendation:

- Encourage Auto-Pay enrollment.
- Offer incentives for digital recurring payments.

---

## 3. Internet Service

Fiber Optic customers churn more frequently than DSL customers.

Possible reasons:

- Higher pricing
- Service quality issues
- Increased customer expectations

Recommendation:

- Improve service quality.
- Offer premium customer support.

---

## 4. Customer Tenure

Customers with lower tenure have significantly higher churn.

Long-term customers are considerably more loyal.

Recommendation:

- Focus retention campaigns during the first year.
- Introduce onboarding and loyalty programs.

---

## 5. Revenue Impact

Customer churn contributes to significant revenue loss.

Reducing churn among high-value customers can substantially improve profitability.

Recommendation:

- Prioritize retention for customers with high monthly charges.

---

## 6. Customer Risk Segmentation

A custom rule-based customer risk model classified customers into:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

The model considers:

- Contract Type
- Tenure
- Technical Support
- Online Security
- Monthly Charges

This enables proactive customer retention.

---

## 7. High-Risk Customers

Customers with:

- Month-to-Month Contracts
- Low Tenure
- No Technical Support
- No Online Security

represent the highest business risk.

Recommendation:

Immediate intervention through personalized retention campaigns.

---

# Strategic Recommendations

1. Increase adoption of annual contracts.

2. Improve customer onboarding during the first year.

3. Promote Technical Support and Online Security add-ons.

4. Target Electronic Check customers with retention offers.

5. Build predictive churn models for proactive customer management.

6. Continuously monitor high-risk customer segments through BI dashboards.

---

# Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that customer churn is driven by multiple business factors rather than a single variable.

Using SQL and Tableau, the project provides actionable insights that can help organizations improve customer retention, reduce revenue loss, and prioritize intervention for high-risk customers through a business-driven risk segmentation framework.
